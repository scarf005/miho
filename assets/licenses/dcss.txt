Ideas and algorithms taken from the Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup source:
- Auto-fight (autofight.lua), auto-explore and travel exclusions.
- Levels: delve (dgn-delve.cc), caves, onion city, mirrored buildings, the Gauntlet.
- Spells: Foxfire, Orb of Destruction, Chain Lightning, Spellforged Servitor, Death Channel and Animate Dead.
- Monsters: bands, followers on the stairs, swap_check, infighting, shouts, mons_should_fire.
- The noise bar, the seed in the options, the message log and the help page layout.
- Descriptions: _monster_stat_description, _monster_attacks_description.
